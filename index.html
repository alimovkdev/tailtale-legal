Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Tailtale is a health diary for dogs, cats and other pets. This page explains what the app does with your data and the terms under which you use it.

Effective September 14, 2026 Applies to Tailtale for iOS, iPadOS and watchOS Language English (other languages to follow)

At a glance

  • No account required. Tailtale works fully offline; nothing is created on our servers to use it.
  • Your records stay yours. Entries live on your device and sync only through your own private iCloud.
  • AI features are opt-in. A photo, recording or note is sent to our AI provider only after you approve that specific request.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no data sold. We don't run ads and don't use the App Tracking Transparency identifier.
  • Export or delete anytime. PDF, CSV and JSON export is always free; deleting a record removes it from your devices and iCloud.

Privacy Policy

What Tailtale collects, why, and who it's shared with.

1Where your data lives

Tailtale is built offline-first. Pet profiles, vaccination and treatment records, weight, symptoms, lab results, visits and documents are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud is signed in, the same records sync to your private CloudKit database — a part of your iCloud storage that only your devices and Apple's infrastructure can read. We do not operate a server that stores your pets' health records, and we cannot read your private CloudKit data.

3AI features and OpenAI

Features marked "AI" — lab report and passport scanning, food label lookup, visit transcription, and chat-style explanations — send only the specific photo, recording or text you submit for that request to our AI provider, OpenAI, through its API. Each of these features asks for your consent the first time you use it, before anything is sent.

Under OpenAI's API terms, content submitted through the API is not used to train their models and is retained only as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, not indefinitely for other purposes. Tailtale does not store AI-extracted results (lab values, passport fields, summaries) until you review and confirm them — a low-confidence quick entry or extraction is discarded if you don't accept it.

Tailtale does not use AI to diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment. AI output is labeled as AI-generated and is informational only — see §4 of the Terms.

4Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; Tailtale never sees your payment details. Purchase state is tracked by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) against an anonymous identifier so the app can unlock the features you've paid for across your devices.

5Advertising and tracking

Tailtale shows no advertising and does not use the App Tracking Transparency identifier (IDFA). We do not sell data and do not share it with data brokers.

6Your controls

  • Export — a full PDF, CSV or JSON export of your data is available at any time, on every plan, at no cost.
  • Delete — deleting a pet, entry or document removes it from your devices and your private iCloud. Some entries use a short-lived "undo" state before they're finally removed.
  • Turn off AI — AI features are opt-in per request; declining consent keeps the app fully usable without them.
  • Turn off iCloud sync — from iOS Settings; your data then stays local to the device.

7Children

Tailtale is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

2What Tailtale is

Tailtale is a personal health diary for pets: it helps you track vaccinations, medication, weight, symptoms, lab results and vet visits, and generate reports from that record. It is a record-keeping and reference tool, not a veterinary service.

3Eligibility

You must be at least 13 years old to use Tailtale. If you're under the age of majority where you live, a parent or guardian should review these terms with you.

4Not veterinary advice

Tailtale, including any AI-generated explanation or summary, does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace a licensed veterinarian. Reference ranges, flags and AI explanations are informational aids for records you already have — always consult a veterinarian for medical decisions about your pet, and treat any note about an urgent or critical value as a prompt to seek care, not as a diagnosis.

Where offered, an introductory price or free trial applies once per subscription per Apple Account. Family Sharing, where enabled for a plan, lets members of your Family Group use the Pro features you've purchased.
